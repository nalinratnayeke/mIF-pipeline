# Production Methods Development Log

## Scope

This is the chronological development and decision log for the production mIF pipeline methods. Current adopted behavior is documented in `METHODS.md`.

Entries here intentionally preserve experiments, diagnostics, failures, provisional settings, and superseded implementations. They are historical evidence and working memory, not a second source of current production truth. When an entry conflicts with `METHODS.md`, the current implementation/configuration and `METHODS.md` take precedence.

InstanSeg model training, training-dataset work, and trained-model comparison history have been separated into `METHODS_TRAINING.md`.

  exact current mask revision, so dependency order and version-isolated output directories remain
  operational requirements.
